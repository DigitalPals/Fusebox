Installed iOS/iPadOS apps recognize a single-finger downward pull that starts
at the top of the document. The indicator changes from **Pull to refresh** to
**Release to refresh** at 80 pixels. Releasing past the threshold reloads the
whole page at its current URL. Android retains its native gesture. Refresh
buttons in both headers and a **Refresh page** command provide alternatives.

Controls, focused editors, nested scrollers, open layers, horizontal/upward
gestures, multi-touch, browser cancellation, navigation and backgrounding do
not trigger a refresh. Drawing is batched once per animation frame and the
spinner respects reduced motion. English and Chinese text are included.

Unsaved Config/YAML edits require an explicit **Reload and discard** confirmation,
with **Keep editing** focused by default. Active saves and playground requests
block refreshing. A bounded, uncached health check keeps the current dashboard
visible if the server is unreachable, and rechecks edits/operations before
navigation. The service worker and backend behavior are unchanged.
